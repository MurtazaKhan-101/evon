Case Study 01 — Real Estate — Dubai, UAE

Dubai Real Estate Agency Triples Qualified Leads in 60 Days

3× Qualified Leads
The Problem

A Dubai agency spent 40+ hours weekly on manual WhatsApp and website inquiries. Sales agents were overwhelmed with unqualified leads, missing follow-ups, and losing hot prospects to faster competitors. Conversion from inquiry to viewing stood at just 8%.

What We Built

A bilingual AI chatbot (English & Arabic) across their website and WhatsApp Business. It qualifies leads by budget, location and buying timeline, then auto-books property viewings into agents' calendars. Unqualified leads enter a nurture sequence.

The Outcome

Within 60 days, qualified leads tripled. Manual follow-up time dropped 70%. Viewing conversion rose from 8% to 27%. The agency closed two additional high-value deals in month one from leads the chatbot caught at 2am.

Case Study 02 — Law Firm — London, UK

London Law Firm Cuts Admin Time by 80% Without Adding Headcount

80% Less Admin
The Problem

A mid-size London law firm was losing 25+ hours weekly to client intake: repetitive questions, collecting case details, scheduling consultations, and chasing document submissions. New client response time averaged 18 hours.

What We Built

A full client intake automation system: an AI assistant handles all first-contact inquiries 24/7, collects case information through structured conversation, sends document request emails, and books consultations with the right solicitor. All data flows directly into their CRM.

The Outcome

Admin time dropped 80%. Response time fell from 18 hours to under 3 minutes. The firm onboarded 34% more new clients in the following quarter without adding headcount. Senior solicitors spent significantly more time on billable work.

Case Study 03 — Marketing Agency — New York, USA

NYC Marketing Agency Generates $42,000 New Revenue in 90 Days

$42K New Revenue
The Problem

A New York agency had strong services but a broken outbound process. Cold emails were generic, reply rates were under 1%, and the founder wrote every message personally. They had capacity for 8 new clients but no consistent pipeline.

What We Built

A fully automated outreach system: AI identifies ideal clients daily, writes hyper-personalised emails referencing each prospect's recent activity, and runs a 5-touchpoint follow-up. Positive replies are instantly flagged and routed to the founder.

The Outcome

Reply rate jumped from under 1% to 6.8%. The agency booked 22 sales calls in 90 days — more than the previous 12 months combined. 7 converted to paying clients generating $42,000 new revenue. Zero manual prospecting time.

Case Study 04 — Medical Clinic — Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Riyadh Medical Clinic Saves 11 Hours Weekly, Cuts No-Shows by 71%

11 Hours Saved Weekly
The Problem

A private Riyadh clinic had two receptionists spending most of their day on appointment booking, reminder calls, and follow-up messages. No-show rates were at 31% due to inconsistent reminders. Patients called repeatedly with the same basic questions.

What We Built

An AI appointment system integrated with their booking software: patients book, reschedule, or cancel via WhatsApp 24/7. Automated reminders run at 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before each appointment. An AI FAQ bot handles all routine patient questions instantly.

The Outcome

Reception staff saved 11 hours weekly. No-show rate dropped from 31% to 9%, recovering significant lost revenue. Patient satisfaction improved as response times dropped to seconds. One receptionist was reassigned to higher-value patient care coordination.
